# C22-l13: Contest Math: Inequalities

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l13-work`

Level `cm-l13`: 3 lessons, a level check of 12 questions, 12 new generators in
`generators/contestInequalities.ts`. Each question turns on one idea: dividing
by a negative turns the sign round (drawn on the number line), a square has two
roots, a square is never negative, a range comes from its corners, a fixed
product makes the sum least and a fixed sum makes the product greatest when the
parts are equal, and Cauchy-Schwarz caps a line on a circle, a sum of squares
on a line, and the fractions form with a fixed sum of bottoms.
