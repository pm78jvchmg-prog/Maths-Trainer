# C19-l3: Hypothesis Testing: Type I and Type II Errors

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c19-hypothesis-l3`

Level `ht-l3`, 5 lessons, a 15-question level check, 19 new generators.

- The Two Mistakes: which error an outcome is, and P(Type I) at most the level
  (`hyp-error-choice`, `hyp-error-flow`, `hyp-error-tiles`, `hyp-error-line`).
- The Size of a Test: P(Type I) as the region's probability under H0, one tail
  or both, for a rule fixed in advance or at three levels (`hyp-size`,
  `hyp-size-sum`, `hyp-size-choice`, `hyp-size-table`).
- Type II Errors: P(Type II) from cumulatives quoted at a stated alternative,
  one-tailed each way (`hyp-beta`, `hyp-beta-choice`, `hyp-beta-flow`).
- Power: 1 minus P(Type II), a further alternative, and the trade between the
  errors at fixed n (`hyp-power`, `hyp-power-pair-tree`, `hyp-power-choice`,
  `hyp-trade-table`).
- Errors in the Mean Test: the critical sample mean, P(Type II) as a normal
  tail with Phi quoted, and a bigger n shrinking it (`hyp-mean-beta`,
  `hyp-mean-miss-tree`, `hyp-beta-n-table`, `hyp-mean-error-choice`).

Level 3 prompts quote cumulatives under an "If $p = ...$:" line so each table
names its p; `hypothesisTesting.test.ts` reads them back and recomputes every
value from the binomial terms, and every Phi by Simpson's rule.
