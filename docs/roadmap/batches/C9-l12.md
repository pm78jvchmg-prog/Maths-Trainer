# C9-l12: Algebraic & Partial Fractions: Turning Points and Ranges of Rational Functions

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-algebraic-fractions-u84jc5`

Level `af-l12` in course `algebraic-fractions`, shown in Partial Fractions &
Rational Functions straight after Graphs of Rational Functions (`af-l5`).
5 lessons (Values a Curve Cannot Take, Turning Points from the
Discriminant, Oblique Asymptotes, Crossing an Asymptote, Putting It
Together), a level check of 15 questions, 20 new generators (`af12-k-quadratic`
to `af12-sketch-flow`) in `generators/fractionsLevel12.ts`.

- Two curve shapes carry the turning points. A quadratic over x + d is built
  as (Ax + e)(x + d) + As^2 over x + d, so the discriminant in k is
  (k - (e - Ad))^2 - (2As)^2 with whole roots and turning points at
  x = -d -/+ s: the lower value a maximum on the left branch, the upper a
  minimum on the right. A quadratic over a never-zero quadratic comes from
  `BOUNDED`, a table searched once at load for whole boundary values and
  whole turning points, with the level strictly between them: the curve takes
  only the values between, the lower a minimum.
- Crossing questions build the top as A times the bottom plus m(x - x0), so
  the x^2 terms cancel and x0 is whole; the never-crossing case adds a
  constant instead, and the slant line of a quadratic over x + d is never
  crossed.
- Widgets: tiles (the quadratic in x with k in it, the factorised condition
  on k, the division into a line and a leftover), numberLine (the range),
  tree (both turning points, the linear equation left at a crossing), flow
  (putting a boundary value back, which side of the slant line, which
  asymptotes can be crossed, planning a sketch), slider on drawn curves (the
  maximum or minimum, the crossing), table (every feature), choice (the
  range, the slant line, the rule from a sketch on squared paper) and typed
  whole numbers (an end of the range, a turning point's x, the line's number
  or the leftover's top, the crossing).
- No generator declares `source` (nothing is differentiated).
  `fractionsLevel12.test.ts` reads the rule off each slide and takes the
  range and turning points from the curve's heights at every whole x, never
  from a discriminant; slant lines from far out, crossings from the bottom
  times the gap to the asymptote. It also plays the typed questions through
  a session and resolves every lesson and the level check over 40 seeds.
