# C22-l17: Contest Math: Modular Arithmetic

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l17-work`

Level `cm-l17`: 4 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 16 new generators in
`generators/contestModular.ts`. Every question replaces a number by its
remainder as early as it can: two or three remainder conditions solved by
listing the larger divisor's numbers, or by one LCM when every remainder is
the same distance short; division modulo a prime by adding the modulus until
the division comes out, with the modulus divided too when it shares a factor;
far digits of a repeating decimal; last digits of towers (the exponent taken
modulo 4), of long sums of powers (a block of ten at a time) and last two
digits from a power ending in 01; φ(n), and huge exponents cut down by p − 1
or φ(n). Every answer is checked by brute force with exact integers. Every
choice form offers the trap: the unreduced remainder, the product for the LCM,
one solution of several, the digit before the shift, a remainder of 0 read as
place 0, the power cut down by p rather than p − 1.
