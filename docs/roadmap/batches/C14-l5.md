# C14-l5: Series Expansions: The Binomial Series

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-three-singles-m94jmr`

Level `se-l5` in course `series-expansions`, after `se-l4`: 5 lessons (Deriving the Binomial Series, The Radius of the Binomial Series, One Coefficient from the Last, Integrating to arctan and arcsin, Binomial Series in Products), a level check of 15 questions, 15 new generators (`ser-binom-*`, in `generators/seriesBinomial.ts`). Takes the Series Expansions card from 24 to 30.

Binomial Expansion's levels 6 and 7 already quote and use the series for rational n, so this level derives it instead: coefficients from the derivatives at 0, the radius by the ratio test and the break where the bracket is 0, the rule taking one coefficient to the next, arctan and arcsin by integrating term by term, and products with the standard series. `seriesBinomial.test.ts` recomputes every answer from the function itself: coefficients by the Cauchy integral round a small circle, radii by walking out along the real line until the function stops being finite, real and of one sign.
