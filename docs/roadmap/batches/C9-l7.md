# C9-l7: Algebraic Fractions: Rational Equations in Context

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-algebraic-fractions-u84jc5`

Level `af-l7` in course `algebraic-fractions`, 5 lessons (Fractions from
Words, Journeys, Working Together, Lenses and Resistors, Prices and
Sharing), a level check of 14 questions, 23 new generators (`af7-word-expr`
to `af7-share-check-tree`) in `generators/fractionsLevel7.ts`.

- Every story is a rate written as a fraction: time is distance over speed,
  the number bought is money over price, a share is a total over the people.
  Lesson 1 stays linear (one fraction equal to a number, or two equal
  fractions); lessons 2, 3 and 5 clear to a quadratic with one root the
  story rejects.
- A journey is drawn by its speed V, the current c and two whole times
  t1, t2. The other root is then c(t1 - t2)/(t1 + t2), drawn only when
  whole and non-zero, so it always lies strictly between -c and c: negative,
  or below the current. The flow asks which of the two it is.
- Work, resistors in parallel and the thin lens share one list of whole
  reciprocal pairs, 1/a + 1/b = 1/t (and 1/a - 1/b = 1/t for a tap and a
  drain; triples for three resistors). Both formulas are given on a teach
  slide and again in each prompt.
- A shared cost or changed price is C/x - C/(x + k) = d, built from its
  answer X with C = dX(X + k)/k and dX divisible by k, so every share,
  count and time in the check is whole. The other root is -(X + k).
- Widgets: choice (expressions and equations from words), tiles (the
  quadratic after clearing), steps (two equal fractions; one over x from a
  difference of reciprocals), tree (checking a speed or a share; rates added
  to a time; resistors), flow (rejecting a journey root), typed whole
  numbers, and `+choice` forms of the typed journey, together, missing
  resistor, lens and share answers.
- No generator declares `source`, `integrand` or `limits` (nothing is
  calculus). `fractionsLevel7.test.ts` reads the numbers back out of each
  story or displayed equation and solves it again: the quadratic formula for
  journeys, longer-time workers and shared costs, reciprocals in floating
  point for work, resistors and lenses. It holds every typed answer, tree
  node, filled quadratic and correct option to that, checks no distractor
  equation is also satisfied by the answer, and checks the rejected root is
  rejected for the right reason. It caught the swapped-distances distractor
  being the same equation when both legs of a journey are equally long.
