# C22-l8: Contest Math: Probability

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l8-work`

Level `cm-l8`: 4 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 18 new generators in
`generators/contestProbability.ts`. Every answer is an exact fraction in
lowest terms typed with the `/` key, and every choice form offers the classic
traps: totals or head-counts treated as equally likely, order forgotten, the
overlap not taken off, "at least one" added up, counters put back, ties
ignored, and the conditional read the wrong way round. Two probability trees
(two taken from a bag, a bag picked by a die) and a Venn diagram of
probabilities carry the working.
