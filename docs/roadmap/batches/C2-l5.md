# C2-l5: Functions & Transformations: Transformations of Trigonometric Graphs

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c2-functions-l5-de6i38`

Level `fn-l5` in course `functions-transformations`, 5 lessons (Amplitude and
Midline, Period as a Stretch, Phase Shift and the Sign Trap, The Equation from
the Graph, Putting It Together), a 15-question level check, 21 new generators:
17 in `functions.ts` (`fun-wave-extremes` to `fun-moves-choice`, two with a
`+choice` form) and two `transform` pairs in `transformGraph.ts`
(`fun-amp-apply`/`-match`, `fun-xstretch-apply`/`-match`, on `sin` only and
only unit-free moves, since the widget's sine runs in radians).

- x is in degrees throughout; every typed, dragged or placed value is whole.
  Rules and chains of moves go through tiles, steps, flows and choices, never
  a typed expression. No slide declares `source`: nothing here is calculus.
- `functions.test.ts` (new) reads each shown or built rule back into a, b, c
  and d, samples the wave every quarter degree, and rechecks every quoted
  maximum, minimum, midline, period, shift and first peak from the samples;
  moves described in words are applied to the sampled `sin x` in order.
- It fired on first run: `fun-phase-choice`, `-steps` and `-flow` could offer
  a second right answer, since a move of h and of bh draw the same curve when
  they differ by whole periods (`cos(3(x + 30))` and `cos(3(x + 270))`), and a
  move of half a period reads as both left and right. `clearShifts` now
  refuses those draws.
