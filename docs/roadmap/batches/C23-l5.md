# C23-l5: Classical Mechanics: Momentum

Status: done
Branch: `claude/classical-mechanics-course-i7qlc4`

Level `clm-l5`, 5 lessons (`clm-l5-impulse`, `clm-l5-bounce`, `clm-l5-2d`,
`clm-l5-rocket`, `clm-l5-gas`) and a 12-question level check; 20 generators
in `generators/classicalMomentum.ts`, with force-time pulses drawn as a
rise, a flat top and a fall.

- Glancing collisions keep only draws that lose no kinetic energy.
- Light and sail forces are in micronewtons, so the numbers stay readable.
- `classicalMomentum.test.ts` adds impulse up slice by slice, steps each
  bounce's rise, conserves momentum per component and burns a rocket's
  fuel a little at a time.
