# C4-l7: Polynomials & the Factor Theorem: Modelling with Polynomials

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c4-polynomials-l7-izp9yj`

Level `pl-l7`, 5 lessons, a 15-question level check, 19 new generators
(`poly-box-*`, `poly-fit-*`, `poly-model-*`), plus `poly-find-lead` and
`poly-sketch-expand-steps` reused in Fitting a Curve.

- The Open Box: V = x(L - 2x)(W - 2x) from a sheet, expanded, and 0 < x < W/2.
- Values and the Table: volumes at whole cuts, a table, the best cut on a slider
  (read off, not differentiated: Differentiation's `df-l8` does that).
- A Given Volume: V = T as a cubic divided by 4, a root by trial, synthetic
  division, and the solution past the end of the sheet thrown out.
- Fitting a Curve: a cubic through its roots and one point, a coefficient from a
  point.
- Reading a Model: what a root, the intercept, a value and a sign say, a cut
  outside the range, and which of four cubics fits a table.

`polynomials.test.ts` rebuilds V(x) from the sheet each prompt states and holds
every slide to it.
