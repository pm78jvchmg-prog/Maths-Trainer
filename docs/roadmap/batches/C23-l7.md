# C23-l7: Classical Mechanics: Statics

Status: done
Branch: `claude/classical-mechanics-course-i7qlc4`

Level `clm-l7`, 5 lessons (`clm-l7-com`, `clm-l7-stack`, `clm-l7-tip`,
`clm-l7-rope`, `clm-l7-body`) and a 12-question level check; 20 generators
in `generators/classicalStatics.ts`.

- Ropes sag along 3-4-5 and 7-24-25 triangles, so every sine is a short
  decimal; weights are in newtons and body lengths in centimetres.
- `classicalStatics.test.ts` checks centres of mass by their moments,
  builds the greatest overhang block by block from the top, and checks the
  body's levers by moments about a second point.
