# C22-l23: Contest Math: Contest Problem Strategies

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l23-work`

Level `cm-l23`: 6 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 25 new generators in
`generators/contestStrategies.ts`. The last level is about how to start: split
into cases, look at the worst case or at what a process never changes, try
small cases until the pattern shows, use a reflection or a pairing, let the
options rule themselves out, and simplify before calculating. Every answer was
checked against a brute force (triangle counts, the cups by a search over how
many are upright, the circle of people by simulation, run-length terms,
largest pair-free sets by trying every subset, both absolute-value solutions,
and the average k-th pick by listing every choice).
