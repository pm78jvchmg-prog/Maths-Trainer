# C22-l10: Contest Math: Factorization

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l10-work`

Level `cm-l10`: 5 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 20 new generators in
`generators/contestFactorization.ts`. Every question turns on a prime
factorisation: topping powers up to a square or a cube, one more choice than
each power for a divisor count, the lower and higher power for the HCF and
LCM, HCF × LCM = a × b, and the 5s in n! a multiple at a time. The
cryptograms factorise place values (AB + BA = 11(A + B), ABC − CBA =
99(A − C), ABCABC = ABC × 7 × 11 × 13) or split a divisor into coprime tests,
and each draw is checked by brute force to have exactly one solution. Every
choice form offers the trap: every prime once, a composite base read as
prime, the product for the LCM, a missing fence post, the reversed number.
