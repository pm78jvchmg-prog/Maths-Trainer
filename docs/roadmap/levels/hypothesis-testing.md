# Hypothesis Testing: level plan

Written by C19.

Has: Testing a Proportion (null and alternative hypotheses and which tail, the
test statistic and its binomial model under H0, p-values from quoted
cumulatives, critical regions and their actual significance level, two-tailed
tests with the level halved); Testing a Mean (the distribution of the sample
mean, the z statistic, the critical values 1.645, 1.96, 2.326 and 2.576, the
decision in context, the level as the chance of rejecting a true H0, and how
the sample size moves the verdict).

Needs: Type I and Type II Errors (the two mistakes, the size of a test, power
for a stated alternative); Testing a Correlation (H0: rho = 0, critical values
of the product-moment coefficient from a quoted table, one- and two-tailed);
Comparing Two Samples (the difference of two means with known variances, and
paired data); Goodness of Fit (the chi-squared statistic, expected
frequencies, degrees of freedom and a quoted critical value); Testing with the
Normal Approximation (large-n binomial tests through the normal, with a
continuity correction; after C18's later levels have landed).
