# C21-l4: Forces & Newton's Laws: Moments

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c21-forces-l4-by5g28`

Level `fo-l4` of course `forces`, 5 lessons (`fo-l4-moment`, `fo-l4-rod`,
`fo-l4-plank`, `fo-l4-tilt`, `fo-l4-ladder`) and a 14-question level check,
20 new `force-*` generators.

- The `forces` widget draws every arrow from one box's centre, so it cannot
  put a force part-way along a rod. The rods, planks and ladders are
  file-local SVG instead: `beamSvg` (a beam to scale on knife-edge supports,
  each force from where it acts, weights down, reactions up, a pull at its
  angle) and `ladderSvg` (a ladder at its true angle against a wall, with W,
  P, S, R and F). Letters only on the pictures, numbers in the prose; points
  are kept a ninth of the beam apart so the letters never collide.
  `beamWindow` gives a slider's marker the span that lines it up with the beam.
- Distances are whole or half metres and forces whole newtons. A reaction or
  distance found by dividing is drawn again through `until` until it is exact;
  a tipping distance must land on the half-metre lattice. Masses appear only
  where g cancels (the greatest mass at an end) or is stated (a mass from a
  given reaction).
- Ladder angles come from the 3-4-5 and 7-24-25 triangles; the least mu is
  exact to three places and under 1.
- `forces.test.ts` checks every force and distance is the one the prompt
  states, then solves the reactions from resolving and moments about A by
  Cramer's rule, finds a tipping point by standing the person there (far
  reaction zero, a quarter metre further negative), and balances the ladder
  by cross products about its foot with the angle from `Math.atan2` of what
  the prompt says. Three mutations (reactions swapped, cot for tan in the
  wall's push, a tipping distance off by a newton metre) and a fourth (every
  moment made clockwise) each turned it red.
