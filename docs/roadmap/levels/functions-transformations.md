# Functions & Transformations: level plan

Written by C2.

Has: Functions; Transformations.

Needs: Graphs of Functions (sketching from key features: intercepts,
asymptotes of 1/x-type graphs, behaviour at the ends, reading f(x) = k as a
horizontal line meeting the curve); Even, Odd and Periodic Functions (f(-x)
against f(x) and -f(x), symmetry in the y-axis and about the origin, period from
the graph and from the rule, piecewise-defined functions); Transformations of
Trigonometric Graphs (y = a sin(bx + c) + d read as stretches and translations,
the phase-shift sign trap, period and amplitude from the equation and from the
graph); Functions in Modelling (choosing a family to fit a context, restricting
the domain to what makes sense, reading a model's inverse and composite in
context); Combining Functions (sums, products and quotients of functions and
their domains, the composite's domain from the inner function's range).

The modulus function, |f(x)| and f(|x|) graphs, and inequalities solved from
graphs belong to C8 Inequalities & the Modulus Function, not here.
