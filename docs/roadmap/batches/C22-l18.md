# C22-l18: Contest Math: Synthetic Geometry

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l18-work`

Level `cm-l18`: 7 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 28 new generators in
`generators/contestSyntheticGeometry.ts`, every one with a figure drawn to
scale from its numbers. The Heron and bisector triangles are fixed lists and
the cyclic quadrilaterals are found at load by whole-number arithmetic; the
figure kit puts each letter in the widest gap between the lines through its
point, so labels stay clear across every draw.
