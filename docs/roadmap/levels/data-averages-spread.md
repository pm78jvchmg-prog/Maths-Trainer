# Data, Averages and Spread: level plan

Written by C17.

Has: Averages and Frequency Tables (mean, median and mode and which one a
question wants, a missing value from a known mean and two groups combined,
frequency tables with an fx column, grouped data by midpoints with an
estimated mean and the modal class, reading a scatter diagram and a line of
best fit); Measures of Spread (the range and what one extreme value does to
it, quartiles and the IQR for lists of length 4k + 3, outliers by the
1.5 times IQR rule, variance from the sum of squares, the standard deviation
and comparing two sets by mean and spread); Representing Data
(stem-and-leaf diagrams with a key and quartiles read from them, box plots
from five values with outliers crossed beyond the 1.5 times IQR fences,
comparing two box plots by median and IQR, histograms with frequency
density over unequal classes, and reading one back: a part of a bar by its
width, the total from the areas, and the tallest bar against the largest
frequency).

Representing Data draws its box plots and histograms as fixed pictures.
Once a box plot widget exists, the Box Plots and Comparing Box Plots
lessons (dat-box-slider, dat-box-read, dat-boxes-*) should move to it, the
learner dragging the five values into place; once a histogram widget
exists, Histograms and Reading a Histogram (dat-fd-slider, dat-fd-area,
dat-hist-part, dat-hist-total) should move to it, the learner setting bar
heights and shading the part of a bar asked about.

Needs: Cumulative Frequency (the curve, reading off the median and quartiles,
percentiles from grouped data, interpolating inside a class); Coding Data
(how adding and multiplying every value moves the mean and the standard
deviation, and the variance from coded sums); Correlation and Regression
(the product moment correlation coefficient, the least-squares line of y on
x, and which variable to predict from); Grouped Variance and Skew (the
standard deviation from a frequency or grouped table, and skew read from
the mean, median and quartiles).
