# Functions & Transformations: level plan

Written by C2.

Has: Functions; Transformations; Graphs of Functions; Even, Odd and Periodic
Functions.

Needs: Transformations of Trigonometric Graphs (y = a sin(bx + c) + d read as
stretches and translations, the phase-shift sign trap, period and amplitude
from the equation and from the graph); Functions in Modelling (choosing a
family to fit a context, restricting the domain to what makes sense, reading a
model's inverse and composite in context); Combining Functions (sums, products
and quotients of functions and their domains, the composite's domain from the
inner function's range).

The modulus function, |f(x)| and f(|x|) graphs, and inequalities solved from
graphs belong to C8 Inequalities & the Modulus Function, not here.
