# C24-l2: Geometry: Polygons

Status: done
Branch: `claude/geometry-course-sdjq9m`

Level `geo-l2`, 3 lessons (`geo-l2-sums`, `geo-l2-exterior`,
`geo-l2-relationships`) and an 11-question level check; 11 generators in
`generators/geometryPolygons.ts`.

- Irregular polygons come from `polygonFromAngles` and are refused when one
  side is under 0.6 of the longest, or two corners' labels crowd together.
- Regular polygons use only side counts whose angles are whole degrees.
- Shapes round a point follow the owner's first inspiration screenshot.
