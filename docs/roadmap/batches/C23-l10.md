# C23-l10: Classical Mechanics: General Considerations

Status: done
Branch: `claude/classical-mechanics-course-i7qlc4`

Level `clm-l10`, 5 lessons (`clm-l10-dims`, `clm-l10-formulas`,
`clm-l10-natural`, `clm-l10-lagrangian`, `clm-l10-euler`) and a
12-question level check; 20 generators in `generators/classicalGeneral.ts`.
This closes the course's ten planned levels.

- Dimensions are powers of M, L and T. Formulas found from dimensions
  solve for three powers by matching each base; halves appear only at the
  harder difficulty.
- Natural time units are 1 / omega for whole or tidy omega, so every
  conversion is exact.
- `classicalGeneral.test.ts` reads dimensions off a change of units,
  scales each law by its full formula, steps the spring or pendulum a
  natural unit comes from, integrates the force for V, and differentiates
  each Lagrangian numerically.
- The owner's design agent checked the formula typesetting before the pull
  request.
