# C9-l10: Algebraic Fractions: Fractions within Fractions

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-algebraic-fractions-u84jc5`

Level `af-l10` in course `algebraic-fractions`, 5 lessons (Numbers First,
Clearing with the Common Bottom, A Fraction over a Fraction, Stacked
Fractions, Equations with a Fraction inside a Fraction), a level check of 15
questions, 20 new generators (`af10-num-tidy-tree` to `af10-eq-quad-tiles`),
two of them with a `+choice` form (`af10-num-lcd`, `af10-stack-value`).
Lessons in `courses/algebraicFractions/level10.ts`, generators in
`generators/fractionsLevel10.ts`.

- Numbers first: the top and the bottom each tidied into one fraction and
  divided, or cleared at a stroke by the smallest number every small bottom
  divides into (never past 12). Fraction answers are picked (tree, choice) or
  placed as whole numbers (tiles), never typed; only that clearing number is
  typed.
- With x: (a + b/B)/(c + d/B) for B = x, or x + k at difficulty 2, multiplied
  through by B; a fraction over a fraction, turned over and multiplied, with
  a quadratic small bottom that factorises and cancels at difficulty 2;
  a + b/(c + d/x) from the inside out; and (a + b/x)/(c + d/x) = N solved,
  with a whole solution by construction.
- Rejection: (x + A/x)/(1 + B/x) = C is built from the roots of the quadratic
  clearing gives. A root of 0 makes the small bottoms zero; a root of -B makes
  the main bottom zero (then the top is zero too, 0/0). Difficulty 1 only
  ever rejects 0; sometimes both roots stand.
- Widgets: tree (tidy and divide; inside out), tiles (cleared numbers, new top
  and bottom, simplified factors, the quadratic), steps (stacked to product
  to answer; equation to one fraction to cleared line to x), flow (how to
  clear, factorise and cancel, where to start, checking roots), choice, and
  typed whole numbers (the clearing number, values at a given x, solutions).
- No generator declares `source`, `integrand` or `limits` (nothing is
  calculus). `fractionsLevel10.test.ts` evaluates what the learner is shown
  with mathjs and holds every answer to it: tidied tops, bottoms and values,
  cleared numbers against the stated multiplier, the clearing number as the
  least common multiple, every simplified form against the original at eight
  points, values at the stated x, typed and stepped solutions in the original
  equation, a rejected root exactly where the original is undefined, and the
  quadratic's roots against the original. Confirmed red on a broken
  `stackTop` and on a rejection that ignored the main bottom.
