# C24-l8: Geometry: Volume

Status: done
Branch: `claude/geometry-course-sdjq9m`

Level `geo-l8`, 3 lessons (`geo-l8-volume`, `geo-l8-cylinders`,
`geo-l8-pointed`) and a 10-question level check; 10 generators in
`generators/geometryVolume.ts`.

- Pyramid and cone volumes are drawn only where a third of base times height
  is whole; sphere radii are multiples of 1.5, so four thirds of r cubed is a
  whole or half number of pi.
- The volume scale factor asks k cubed, the cube root back, and the volume of
  a similar solid.
