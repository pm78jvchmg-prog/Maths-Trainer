# C21-l6: Forces & Newton's Laws: Work, Energy and Power

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c21-forces-l6-7qassh`

Level `fo-l6` of course `forces`, after Momentum and Impulse: 5 lessons
(`fo-l6-work`, `fo-l6-energy`, `fo-l6-work-energy`, `fo-l6-friction`,
`fo-l6-power`) and a 15-question level check, 20 new `force-*` generators.

- Masses are whole kilograms or halves (vehicles whole hundreds), speeds whole
  metres per second, distances and heights whole or half metres, given forces
  whole newtons, and g = 9.8 is stated wherever it is used. Angles on a pull or
  a slope come from 3-4-5 and 7-24-25 triangles; a hill is `sin(alpha) = 1/k`
  with k 14, 28 or 49, so mg sin(alpha) is whole.
- No speed found from energy is a rounded root. With g = 9.8 a fall changes
  v^2 by 19.6h, which is tidy only when 49 divides the difference of squares,
  so the falls, stops, skids and pulls on a rough floor come from catalogues
  built once (`FALLS`, `STOPS`, `SKIDS`, `HAULS`); elsewhere the speeds are
  drawn first and the force or distance is built from them through `until`.
- The `forces` kind shows no distance moved, so pictures are `pullSvg` (a rope
  at an angle, the distance marked), `dropSvg` (a fall from a ledge, the
  height marked) and `slopeSvg`.
- The work-energy tiles go through `onlyAnswer` / `soleExtras`, and the work
  done is one tile, never a product of two, since `P x d` commutes.
- `forces.test.ts` reads every mass, speed, force, distance, energy, power, mu
  and angle off the rendered prose and re-forms each answer from W = Fd cos a,
  mgh, half m v^2, mu R d and P = Fv, checking every speed from energy is whole
  and every stopping distance is on the half-metre lattice. Six planted slips
  (sine for cosine in the work, h = (v^2 - u^2)/g, friction without the cosine
  on a slope, half the hill's pull, the pull's sign in `HAULS`, KE without the
  half) each turned it red.
- Variable Forces is the next level; momentum is level 5's and the scalar
  product stays `vm-l1-dot`.
