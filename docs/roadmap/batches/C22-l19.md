# C22-l19: Contest Math: Analytical Geometry

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l19-work`

Level `cm-l19`: 4 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 16 new generators in
`generators/contestAnalyticGeometry.ts`. Level 5 already asks distance,
midpoints, collinearity and the shoelace; this level goes past them with one
idea per question: reflect an end so a bent walk becomes straight, count
lattice points with a gcd and then Pick's theorem, find a height by working an
area out twice, add the distances to an ellipse's foci, measure a parabola's
focal distance to its directrix, balance a triangle with mass points, and turn
with `i` on the complex plane (the `plot` widget). Figures for the walk, the
lattice triangle, the ellipse and every mass-point triangle are drawn to scale.
