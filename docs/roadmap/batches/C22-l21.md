# C22-l21: Contest Math: More Advanced Combinatorics

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l21-work`

Level `cm-l21`: 5 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 20 new generators in
`generators/contestAdvancedCounting.ts`. Each question goes a step past Level 7
and Level 11: place the others first and drop the kept-apart ones into the
gaps, let a chosen set of digits fix its own order, count the opposite (no 7,
all different, routes through a closed corner, teams with none), read a
coefficient as a choice of brackets and a diagonal of Pascal's triangle as the
entry below it, run inclusion–exclusion over three sets, derangements and
shares that leave nobody out, and share identical things with minimums, odd
parts, an upper limit or a slack variable. Every answer was checked against a
brute force over the sampled draws.
