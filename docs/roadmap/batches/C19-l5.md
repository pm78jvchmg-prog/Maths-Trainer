# C19-l5: Hypothesis Testing: Comparing Two Samples

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-three-singles-m94jmr`

Level `ht-l5` in course `hypothesis-testing`, after `ht-l4`: 5 lessons (Two Means, One Difference, The Two-Sample z Statistic, Deciding in Context, Paired Data, Testing Paired Differences), a level check of 15 questions, 20 new generators (`hyp-diff-*` for two independent samples, `hyp-paired-*` for paired data), three of them with a `+choice` form in use.

Every `\sigma^2 / n` is whole and the two add to a square, so the standard deviation of `\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B` is whole and z has at most two places; paired draws keep `\sigma_d / \sqrt{n}` whole. No z lands within 0.05 of its critical value. A suspicion may name either group first ("B is lighter" is `H_1: \mu_A > \mu_B`), and a paired question may take d either way round. `hypothesisTesting.test.ts` reads every table, mean, sigma, n and suspicion back out of the prompt's TeX and works each answer, tail and decision again from those.
