# C20-l3: Kinematics: Calculus in Kinematics

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c20-kinematics-l3`

Level `kn-l3` in course `kinematics`, 5 lessons (Velocity from Displacement,
Acceleration, Exponential Motion, Back by Integrating, Displacement over an
Interval), a level check of 14 questions, 24 new generators (`kin-ds-dt` to
`kin-area-slider`), six of them with a `+choice` form.

- Answers typed as a formula in t use a file-local `T_KEYS` (and `T_EXP_KEYS`
  with `e`); the "every kinematics answer" test skips expression slides whose
  keypad holds `t`, since those answers are formulas rather than numbers.
- No generator declares `source`. Only the definite integrals (`kin-disp-int`,
  `kin-dist-int`) declare `integrand`, with `limits` and t renamed to x.
- `kinematics.test.ts` reads each displayed formula off the slide and checks
  every derivative with mathjs in t and every integral by Simpson's rule.
