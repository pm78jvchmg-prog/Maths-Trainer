# C19-l4: Hypothesis Testing: Testing a Correlation

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c19-hypothesis-l4-eyljh5`

Level `ht-l4` in course `hypothesis-testing`, after `ht-l3`: 5 lessons (Hypotheses about Rho, The Critical Value, Testing One Way, Testing Either Way, Sample Size and Level), a level check of 15 questions, 20 new generators (`hyp-rho-*`, `hyp-pmcc-*`).

Every critical value is quoted from a file-local `PMCC_TABLE` (n = 4 to 30, one-tailed 10% to 0.5%, four places), at most three columns to an excerpt so it fits a phone. `hypothesisTesting.test.ts` recomputes each quoted value from the density of r under H0 by quadrature, checks the columns fall and the rows rise, and rederives every decision from the quoted r, its sign and the critical value.
