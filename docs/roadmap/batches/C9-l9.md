# C9-l9: Algebraic Fractions: Rearranging Formulae with Fractions

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-algebraic-fractions-u84jc5`

Level `af-l9` in course `algebraic-fractions`, 5 lessons (The Subject on the
Bottom, The Subject Twice, Inverse Functions of Fractions, Sums of
Reciprocals, Rearrange Then Substitute), a level check of 15 questions, 21
new generators (`af9-bottom-letters` to `af9-slip-flow`) in
`generators/fractionsLevel9.ts`.

- A rearranged formula is a form, so no rearrangement is typed: they go
  through choice (physics formulae, inverses, self-inverse functions, the
  equal form collected on the right, the first wrong line), tiles (top and
  bottom of the subject), steps (the key method line by line, the inverse,
  sums of reciprocals), tree (bracket and other side, numbers into
  R = R1R2/(R1 + R2) and the lens formula, rearrange-then-substitute) and flow
  (planning y = k/(x + b) + c, checking an attempt line by line). Only whole
  numbers are typed: f^{-1}(k), the value f^{-1} is undefined at, one of a
  reciprocal pair, and a letter of I = V/(R + r), a = (v - u)/t, the lens
  formula or s = (u + v)t/2.
- The pure rearrangements collect the subject's terms on the left, which
  fixes the signs of the top and the bottom. `safeSlips` drops any tile that
  would pair with another into a fraction equal to the answer, so the form
  collected on the right can never be placed and marked wrong.
- Every whole answer is built outward: f^{-1}(k) and the tree's x from the x
  first, the reciprocal pairs as X + e and X + X^2/e for e dividing X^2.
- `fractionsLevel9.test.ts` parses the formula and the claimed answer off each
  slide and puts the answer back: the x found returns the y, f of the inverse
  is x, the letter found satisfies the formula with random values for the
  rest, the first line false on the curve is the line picked.
