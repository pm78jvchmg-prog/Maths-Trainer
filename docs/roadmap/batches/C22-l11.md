# C22-l11: Contest Math: Reframing Problems

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l11-work`

Level `cm-l11`: 4 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 19 new generators in
`generators/contestReframing.ts`. Each question is answered by swapping it for
an easier one: one match per player knocked out, a route as a word of rights
and ups, a share as sweets and dividers, the story run backwards, the last day
drawn, cubes sorted by where they sit, and digit sums that keep a number's
remainder on division by 9. Every answer was checked against a brute force
(route counts, shares, dice in all 24 orientations, the red-cube placements,
self-describing numbers of 4 to 10 digits), and typed look-and-say answers stay
at seven digits so the grader's tolerance cannot pass a wrong digit.
