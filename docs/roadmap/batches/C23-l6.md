# C23-l6: Classical Mechanics: Frames of Reference

Status: done
Branch: `claude/classical-mechanics-course-i7qlc4`

Level `clm-l6`, 5 lessons (`clm-l6-relative`, `clm-l6-com`,
`clm-l6-rotating`, `clm-l6-dilation`, `clm-l6-length`) and a 12-question
level check; 20 generators in `generators/classicalFrames.ts`.

- Relativity uses a stated gamma or 0.6c and 0.8c, whose gammas are 1.25
  and 5/3; light covers 300 m per microsecond.
- Relativistic speed addition has few exact decimal answers, so the typed
  question keeps to the pairs that have one and the flow question offers
  the answer as a fraction in lowest terms.
- `classicalFrames.test.ts` steps a boat across a river, checks zero
  momentum in the centre-of-mass frame and energy kept in an elastic bounce,
  and checks relativity by the invariant interval and by adding rapidities.
