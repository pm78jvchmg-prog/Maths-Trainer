# C18-l6: Binomial & Normal Distributions: The Distribution of the Sample Mean

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c18-binomial-normal-l6-g3619a`

Level `bn-l6` in course `binomial-normal`, 5 lessons (A Total Divided by n, N(μ, σ²/n) and σ/√n, How the Spread Shrinks, A Probability for a Mean, Working Back from a Mean), a level check of 15 questions, 20 new generators (`dist-mean-*`, `dist-xbar-*`, `dist-shrink-*`, `dist-one-vs-mean`, `dist-min-n*`).

Level 5 had landed on main when this started, so it branches from main.

- Every draw takes n from `SQUARE_COUNTS` and σ from `TOTAL_SIGMAS` with √n dividing σ and σ/√n at least 2, so Var(X̄) and σ_X̄ are whole and never equal. Boundaries come from level 5's `drawOffset` against σ/√n, so z has at most two places; the one-value comparison also refuses a boundary whose z against σ has more.
- The smallest n is found in whole numbers (z in thousandths, squared), so no float decides a boundary; distances are 2-and-5-smooth so z σ / d always terminates for the working.
- Hypothesis Testing level 2 (`ht-l2-sample-mean`, `ht-l2-z`) quotes the result and tests with it; this level derives it, stops at the probability, and points there.
- `binomialNormal.test.ts` reads μ, σ, n, every boundary and every quoted Φ back out of each prompt, works σ²/n and σ/√n itself, holds each probability to the quoted Φ and to Simpson within 0.001, and puts each worked-back n or k back in. Four planted mistakes (σ for σ/√n when standardising, σ²/n² for Var(X̄), a floored n, the wrong factor marked in the shrink choice) each turned it red.
- `sampleMeanSvg` draws one value of X dashed with the mean of each sample size over it, for the teach slides.
