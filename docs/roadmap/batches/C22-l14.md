# C22-l14: Contest Math: Polynomials

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l14-work`

Level `cm-l14`: 4 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 17 new generators in
`generators/contestPolynomials.ts`. Each question is answered without solving
the polynomial: the remainder is one substitution, p(1) adds the coefficients,
two quadratics sharing a root are subtracted, a quartic in even powers is a
quadratic in x², and Vieta's formulas give sums, products and the coefficients
of a polynomial with scaled, squared or shifted roots. The questions on sums
of squares, reciprocals and cubes of a quadratic's roots draw only roots that
are real but not whole, so solving is never the quicker way in.
