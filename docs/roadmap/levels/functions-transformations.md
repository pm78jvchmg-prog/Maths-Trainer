# Functions & Transformations: level plan

Written by C2.

Has: Functions; Transformations; Graphs of Functions; Even, Odd and Periodic
Functions; Transformations of Trigonometric Graphs.

Needs: Functions in Modelling (choosing a family to fit a context,
restricting the domain to what makes sense, reading a model's inverse and
composite in context); Combining Functions (sums, products and quotients of
functions and their domains, the composite's domain from the inner function's
range).

The modulus function, |f(x)| and f(|x|) graphs, and inequalities solved from
graphs belong to C8 Inequalities & the Modulus Function, not here.
