# C21: Forces & Newton's Laws

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c21-forces-l1-l2-83qzpc`

Course `forces` in the Mechanics tab (position 20, after Kinematics), levels
`fo-l1` Forces and Equilibrium and `fo-l2` Newton's Laws, 10 lessons, level
checks of 14 and 14 questions, 46 new `force-*` generators. Every angle comes
from a 3-4-5 or 7-24-25 triangle so answers are exact decimals; g = 9.8 is
stated in each question that uses it. `forces.test.ts` checks every rendered
answer against the mechanics worked out independently. Free-body diagrams are
teach figures; the level plan names the five lessons that should later gain a
question on the `forces` diagram widget.
