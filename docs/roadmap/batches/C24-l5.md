# C24-l5: Geometry: Areas

Status: done
Branch: `claude/geometry-course-sdjq9m`

Level `geo-l5`, 4 lessons (`geo-l5-reasoning`, `geo-l5-polygons`,
`geo-l5-circles`, `geo-l5-scaling`) and a 13-question level check; 16
generators in `generators/geometryAreas.ts`.

- Every height is drawn dashed with its right-angle square; an obtuse
  triangle's height falls outside it, with the base run on dashed to meet it.
- A parallelogram's sloping side is labelled at the harder level, so the
  learner has to leave it out.
- Circle areas are in terms of π, typed with the π key; a half-integer radius
  comes from an odd diameter.
- The formula card on the Circle Areas lesson lists every area formula taught.
