# C2-l6: Functions & Transformations: Functions in Modelling

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c2-functions-l6-5m3rkt`

Level `fn-l6` in course `functions-transformations`, 5 lessons (Which Family
Fits, The Rule from the Story, The Domain That Makes Sense, The Inverse in
Context, The Composite in Context), a 15-question level check, 20 new
generators in `functions.ts` (`fun-model-family` to `fun-composite-tiles`,
three with a `+choice` form).

- Four model families (linear up, linear down, area `x(k - x)`, reciprocal
  `K/x`) across 26 settings, plus six growth, six conversion and seven price
  settings, so no word-problem family has fewer than six stories. Every value
  is whole or a tenth; units stay in the prose. No slide declares `source`.
- `functions.test.ts` rebuilds each model from the story's own words and
  numbers, independently of the generator, and rechecks every stated rule,
  value, domain, range, inverse, family sketch (least-squares fit of the
  drawn path) and composite order.
- Ten deliberate bugs were planted one at a time (wrong gradient, swapped
  composite order, off-by-one domain end, and so on); every one turned the
  checks red.
- Browser pass: the composite worked solutions ran off a 393 px screen and
  now break over two lines.
