# C9-l6: Algebraic & Partial Fractions: The Method of Differences

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c9-fractions-l6-9d44re`

Level `af-l6` in course `algebraic-fractions`, 5 lessons (The Split That
Telescopes, A Number in Front, Three Factors, Starting Later and a Top in r,
The Sum to Infinity), a level check of 15 questions, 21 new generators
(`frac-diff-cover` to `frac-within`).

- Sequences & Series (`sq-l4-telescoping`, `sq-l4-infinity`) cancels these
  sums with the split given; this level finds the split and uses it. Teach
  slides point there for the cancelling and the limit.
- Every sum is a `Series`: a pair of brackets g apart, three factors in a row,
  or k(2r + 2a + 1) over two squares. Partial sums, what survives and limits
  are exact fractions from the surviving pieces, written through `exactTex`.
- Widgets: tiles (the split, the number in front, what survives, the regrouped
  three-factor split), tree (cover-up, the limit from the front pieces), table
  (partial sums and S_n), flow (planning a sum, the sum to infinity), choice,
  and typed whole numbers (a numerator, n from a given sum, how many terms
  bring the partial sum within a distance of the limit).
- No generator declares `source`, `integrand` or `limits` (nothing is
  calculus). The new `describe` in `algebraicFractions.test.ts` reads each
  term and split off the slide, finds numerators from the term beside each
  bracket, adds the terms up itself, and holds every partial sum, closed form,
  n, limit and fewest-terms answer to those sums. `toMath` there now also
  reads r and n, which only level 6 writes.
