# C22-l22: Contest Math: More Advanced Probability

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l22-work`

Level `cm-l22`: 6 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 24 new generators in
`generators/contestAdvancedProbability.ts`. Every answer is exact: a fraction
in lowest terms typed with the `/` key, or a whole number or terminating
decimal. Each lesson goes a step past Level 8: two random numbers as a point
in a square, "exactly" as a difference of "at most"s (and inclusion and
exclusion for the smallest and largest together), a test result counted in a
crowd of 1000 or 10000, a coin weighed by its tosses, expected values read
backwards and from their tail chances, re-spins decided by working backwards,
answers written in terms of themselves, indicators for different faces and
mixed neighbours, runs built one success at a time, a race of patterns, and
the gambler's ruin with steps in a ratio. Every choice form offers the naive
answer as its trap.
