# Numerical Methods: level plan

Written by C15.

Has: Locating Roots and Iteration (sign change and when it fails, rearranging
to x = g(x), staircase and cobweb diagrams, |g'(α)| < 1); Newton-Raphson and
the Trapezium Rule (the tangent step, stopping and proving accuracy with a sign
change, where Newton-Raphson fails, trapezia and over- or under-estimates);
Bounds and Errors (absolute and relative error, rounding bounds on a result,
how an error in x_n carries through g).

Needs: Simpson's Rule (pairs of strips under a parabola, why n is even,
comparing it with the trapezium rule on the same ordinates); Euler's Method
(stepping dy/dx = f(x, y) one tangent at a time, step size against error; after
C13 Differential Equations has landed); Choosing a Method (bisection, iteration
and Newton-Raphson side by side, speed of convergence, which to reach for and
when each breaks); Numerical Differentiation (forward and central differences,
and why the central one is better).
