# C21-l5: Forces & Newton's Laws: Momentum and Impulse

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c21-forces-l5-1qzcnm`

Level `fo-l5` of course `forces`, after Moments: 5 lessons (`fo-l5-momentum`,
`fo-l5-conservation`, `fo-l5-coalesce`, `fo-l5-impulse`, `fo-l5-force-time`)
and a 15-question level check, 20 new `force-*` generators.

- Right is positive in every question, said once; a velocity to the left is
  negative and reaches the prose only through `moving()` ("moving left at
  ..."), so the tests read every velocity back the same way. Masses are whole
  kilograms or halves, velocities whole metres per second. A velocity after a
  collision, a common velocity or a force from Ft = mv - mu is built from the
  rest and drawn again through `until` until it is whole or exact.
- `collisionOf` refuses a collision that could not happen: A must catch B, they
  may not pass through each other, and no kinetic energy may appear. A wall or
  barrier never sends a ball back faster than it came; a bat may.
- The `forces` kind draws one box, so collisions are drawn by `collisionSvg`:
  a row for before and a row for after, each box with its mass and velocity
  arrow, an unknown dashed with its letter, two touching boxes for a push
  apart from rest, one joined box after coalescing.
- Tiles banks for equations and vectors go through `onlyAnswer` /
  `soleExtras`: a distractor is kept only if no other choice of tiles also
  makes the statement true (adding momenta commutes, and a tile that happens
  to fit would be a right answer marked wrong). Blanks that follow a term only
  take a signed tile.
- `forces.test.ts` reads each mass, velocity, momentum, impulse, force and time
  off the rendered prose, tables and vectors, and re-forms every answer from
  m times v and F times t: conservation for the unknown velocity, the tiles
  equation evaluated with the true velocity, the steps line's value at every
  stage, the table rows, the flow paths. Three planted slips (a sign on B's
  momentum in `collisionOf`, u + v for v - u in the impulse, total + qB in the
  flow) each turned it red.
- Work and energy are the next level; a dropped object's impact speed stays
  Kinematics' `kn-l2-more`.
