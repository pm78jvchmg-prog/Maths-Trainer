# C23-l9: Classical Mechanics: Oscillations

Status: done
Branch: `claude/classical-mechanics-course-i7qlc4`

Level `clm-l9`, 5 lessons (`clm-l9-molecule`, `clm-l9-damping`,
`clm-l9-coupled`, `clm-l9-waves`, `clm-l9-standing`) and a 12-question
level check; 20 generators in `generators/classicalOscillations.ts`.

- Angular frequencies are whole, set by choosing each stiffness as a mass
  times a square; wave speeds are whole and tensions are mu v^2.
- `classicalOscillations.test.ts` steps two masses on a spring and coupled
  pairs mass by mass, reads damping off the peaks of a stepped damped
  spring, and treats a string as a chain of 4000 beads, whose normal modes
  approach the continuous string's.
