# C3-l6: Coordinate Geometry: Areas and Loci

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c3-coordinate-l6-v4yd9y`

Level `cg-l6` in course `coordinate-geometry`, 5 lessons (Base and Height,
The Box and the Shoelace, Polygons, A Locus as an Equation, Loci from a
Condition), a level check of 15 questions, 24 new generators
(`coord-tri-area`, `coord-base-height-tree`, `coord-half-base-choice`,
`coord-apex-*`, `coord-box-*`, `coord-shoelace-tree`,
`coord-quad-split-tree`, `coord-para-area`, `coord-poly-shoelace-steps`,
`coord-area-k-tiles`, `coord-locus-*`). Every figure is built from its
answer: a level-sided triangle from a base and height with an even product,
a triangle with no level side from the box round it with `wh - ts` even, a
quadrilateral kept only when its shoelace sum is even, the locus PA = 2PB
from its centre and a whole step w (B = O - w, A = O - 4w, radius 2|w|).
Figures are SVG polygons on squared paper (`shapeFigure`), since `plotSvg`
cannot draw a side straight up. `coordinateGeometry.test.ts` reworks every
area by its own shoelace and tests every locus against a half-unit grid and
the condition as stated. Moving Lines and Circles is the next level.
