# Coordinate Geometry: level plan

Written by C3.

Has: Straight Lines, Midpoints, Distances and Circles, Lines Meeting Circles,
Circle Theorems on Axes.

Needs: Coordinate Proof (showing a quadrilateral is a parallelogram,
rectangle, rhombus or square, or a triangle right-angled or isosceles, from
gradients and distances); Areas and Loci (areas of triangles and polygons on
axes, and a locus written as an equation); Moving Lines and Circles
(translating and reflecting their equations, with the C2 graph widget).

Every level keeps its points on the lattice and takes its distances from the
triples table. Where a line meets a parabola stays in Quadratics `qd-l4`.
