# C9-l11: Algebraic & Partial Fractions: Partial Fractions with Higher Powers

Status: done
Branch: `claude/under30-algebraic-fractions-u84jc5`

Level `af-l11` in course `algebraic-fractions`, shown in Partial Fractions &
Rational Functions after Quadratic Factors in the Denominator. 5 lessons (A
Cubed Factor, A Squared Factor with Two Others, Powers of x, Repeated Factors
with a Whole Part, Choosing the Form), a level check of 15 questions, 25 new
generators (`af11-cubed-form` to `af11-choose-value`) in
`generators/fractionsLevel11.ts`.

- Every bottom is a list of factors, `(x + c)^n` (a power of x when c = 0) or
  an irreducible `x^2 + px + q`, and every fraction is built from its split by
  `topOf`: the numerators are drawn first and multiplied up, so each one the
  learner finds is whole.
- The cubed factor is taught two ways: C at x = -a, then A and B from the
  coefficients; and the top rewritten in u = x + a, then divided by u^3.
- Widgets: choice (the form), tree (cover-up then coefficients), steps (the
  shift to u, cover-up through a squared bracket, divide then split), tiles
  (numerators, whole part and parts, the form built from its parts), flow
  (what x = 0 isolates, whole part or not, whether a quadratic splits), and
  typed whole numbers (one numerator, the count of unknowns).
- No generator declares `source`, `integrand` or `limits` (nothing is
  calculus). `fractionsLevel11.test.ts` reads the fraction and the form with
  letters off each slide, finds every numerator again by solving a linear
  system at sample points, and holds each claimed answer to it. A right form
  must fit the fraction exactly and every offered wrong form must not; the
  choose-the-form questions are checked against a made-up top over the bottom
  shown.
