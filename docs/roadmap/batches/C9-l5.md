# C9-l5: Algebraic & Partial Fractions: Graphs of Rational Functions

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c9-fractions-l5-5ybux6`

Level `af-l5` in course `algebraic-fractions`, 5 lessons (Vertical Asymptotes,
Horizontal Asymptotes, Holes, Intercepts, The Sketch), a level check of 15
questions, 20 new generators (`frac-va-which` to `frac-sketch-flow`).

- Every fraction is a `Rational`: a lead and whole roots for each line, so
  every pole, hole, intercept and asymptote is whole. Shared roots are holes.
- Widgets: slider (a pole by its arms, the level line, a hole the graph
  cannot show, the y-intercept), tiles (limit notation either side of a pole,
  the simplified form and the missing value), flow, tree, choice, a feature
  `table`, and typed numbers (the asymptote's height, where the curve crosses
  it). `frac-sketch-which` draws the curve on squared paper and offers four
  rules, each wrong one differing in a feature the grid shows.
- The course's `teach` now takes any `Block`, with a `graph` builder on
  `plotSvg` modelled on `brokenGraph`.
- No generator declares `source` (nothing here is calculus). The new
  `describe` in `algebraicFractions.test.ts` reads each rule off the slide,
  finds poles, holes, the level far out and the intercepts by evaluation, and
  holds every quoted feature to them; the sketch test reads the drawn curve
  back off the SVG grid and requires exactly one option to fit it.
