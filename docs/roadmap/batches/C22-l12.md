# C22-l12: Contest Math: More Advanced Algebra

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l12-work`

Level `cm-l12`: 6 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 25 new generators in
`generators/contestAdvancedAlgebra.ts`. Each question gives way to one move:
factorise a difference of squares, add or subtract whole equations, time the
dog instead of following it, add rates rather than times, complete the square,
subtract two quadratics to find their common root, find a copy of an endless
root inside itself, put both sides over one base, take out the smallest power,
group floors by value, read absolute values as distances, substitute twice,
pair the terms from the two ends, and change every logarithm to one base.
Every choice form offers the trap the naive method gives (the average of two
speeds, the constant term as the least value, the number of factors in a log
chain).
