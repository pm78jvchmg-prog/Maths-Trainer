# C22-l15: Contest Math: Sequences and Series

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l15-work`

Level `cm-l15`: 3 lessons, a level check of 12 questions, 17 new generators in
`generators/contestSequencesSeries.ts`. A step past Level 2's sequences: places
that add to the same number, three terms named round the middle, terms read
off a rule for the sums, block sums, the geometric mean against the average,
multiply-and-subtract for a geometric sum, a series that goes on for ever as a
copy of itself, and series that telescope through fractions, roots and
factorials. Every answer was checked against the series added term by term.
