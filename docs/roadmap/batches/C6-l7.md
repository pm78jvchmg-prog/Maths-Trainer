# C6-l7: Trigonometric Identities & Equations: General Solutions & Harder Equations

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c6-trig-identities-l7-9stv5o`

Level `ti-l7`, 5 lessons, a 14-question level check, 14 new generators
(`tid-general-*`, three with a derived `+choice` form). General solutions
(`α + 360°n`, `±α + 360°n`, `α + 180°n`, and `α + 2nπ` to `α + nπ` in
radians) go through tiles, table, tree, steps, flow and choice, never typed;
typed answers are an angle or a count. Lessons: every solution of sin x = k
and cos x = k; tan x = k and radians; a bracket, divided through turn and all;
harder equations (an identity, a double angle or a factor formula first); and
choosing the route, including what dividing by sin x or cos x loses.
`trigIdentities.test.ts` reads each family off the TeX and holds it to a
one-degree scan of the equation.
