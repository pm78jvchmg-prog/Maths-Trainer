# C22-l9: Contest Math: Fast Problem-Solving

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l9-work`

Level `cm-l9`: 5 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 20 new generators in
`generators/contestFast.ts`. Mental shortcuts that beat long arithmetic:
friendly pairs brought together, numbers near a round one, squares ending in 5
and their same-tens cousins, powers compared through a common exponent, 2s and
5s paired into tens, roots split and nested roots undone, and chains of
operations run backwards. The difference of squares around a number ending in
5 is worked on a `steps` slide, and every choice form offers the slip the long
way invites: the bigger exponent taken as the bigger number, a root of a sum
split into a sum of roots, the reversed number, "it depends on the number".
