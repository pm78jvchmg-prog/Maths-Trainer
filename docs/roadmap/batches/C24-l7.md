# C24-l7: Geometry: Surface Area

Status: done
Branch: `claude/geometry-course-sdjq9m`

Level `geo-l7`, 3 lessons (`geo-l7-surface`, `geo-l7-shortcut`,
`geo-l7-pyramids`) and a 10-question level check; 10 generators in
`generators/geometrySolids.ts`, which also draws every solid Level 8 uses.

- Solids are drawn in oblique projection, hidden edges dashed; round solids
  use an ellipse per circle with its back half dashed.
- Shapes at the ends of the ranges are drawn within set proportions rather
  than to scale, so labels have room; the given lengths are unchanged.
- The harder pyramid and cone questions give the vertical height, so the
  slant height comes from a Pythagorean triple first.
- The prism tree is `geo-shortcut-tree`, a family of its own.
