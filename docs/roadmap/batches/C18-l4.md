# C18-l4: Binomial & Normal Distributions: Normal Approximation to the Binomial

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c18-binomial-normal-l4-dlwr5d`

Level `bn-l4` in course `binomial-normal`, 5 lessons, a level check of 15 questions, 20 new generators (`dist-approx-*` and `dist-cc-*`).

Level 3 (Finding Both μ and σ) was still on its branch when this started, so `bn-l4` follows `bn-l2` in the course file; level ids are fixed, so nothing is renumbered when level 3 lands.

- n and p come from `SQUARE_PAIRS`, whose variance np(1 - p) is a perfect square, so σ is whole. A route question uses only the pairs whose σ has at most one factor of 2, since a σ of 4 or 8 never gives a corrected z of two places, and refuses any boundary whose z has more.
- The bars under the curve are file-local SVG (`barsSvg`), in the same box as `plotSvg` so a slider's marker lines up with them.
- `dist-cc-line` asks the continuity correction on the number line, with every end hollow: the prompt says so, since Y is continuous and an end carries no probability.
- `binomialNormal.test.ts` reads n, p, the event and every quoted Φ back out of each prompt, works the correction out bar by bar, and holds each approximation within 0.015 of the exact binomial sum.
