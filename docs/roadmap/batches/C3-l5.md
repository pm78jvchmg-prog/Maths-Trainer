# C3-l5: Coordinate Geometry: Coordinate Proof

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c3-coordinate-l5-rzj3vk`

Level `cg-l5` in course `coordinate-geometry`, 5 lessons (Parallel Sides,
Right Angles, Equal Sides, Triangles, Writing the Proof), a level check of 15
questions, 19 new generators (`coord-para-*`, `coord-parallel-k`,
`coord-rect-*`, `coord-diagonals-tree`, `coord-side-lengths-tiles`,
`coord-rhombus-flow`, `coord-name-quad`, `coord-square-steps`, `coord-tri-*`,
`coord-isosceles-flow`, `coord-proof-order`, `coord-complete-choice`,
`coord-fails-one-choice`). Every figure is built from its verdict: a
parallelogram from a corner and two lattice vectors, a rhombus from two
`latticeVectors` of one squared length, a right angle from a vector and its
perpendicular. `coord-proof-order` is an `order` slide and is exported as
`coordinateGeometryOrders` for `proofOrder.test.ts`. No areas: those are the
Areas and Loci level.
