# C22-l20: Contest Math: Trigonometry

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l20-work`

Level `cm-l20`: 5 lessons, a level check of 14 questions, 20 new generators in
`generators/contestTrigonometry.ts`. Every value is exact, held as c√r / d, so
a surd is typed with the √( key and a fraction with /. The whole triangles
with a 60° or 120° angle, the parallelograms with whole diagonals and the
triangles with a whole median are searched for when the file loads rather
than listed, and the figures (a triangle to scale, one in its circle, a
parallelogram, a median) keep their labels clear of every line they could
meet.
