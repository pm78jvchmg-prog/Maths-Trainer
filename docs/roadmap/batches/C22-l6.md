# C22-l6: Contest Math: Composite Figures

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l6-work`

Level `cm-l6`: 5 lessons, a level check of 13 questions, 20 new generators in
`generators/contestComposite.ts`, each with an SVG figure (a path round a
lawn, a turned square at a centre, a window and a cut plate, a leaf, an
arbelos, Hippocrates' lunes, an incircle, a square in a semicircle, an
equilateral triangle between two circles, a ring and its chord, a point in a
rectangle, a striped triangle, a hexagon, a square cut by a diagonal, painted
and drilled cubes, a tower of cubes). Curved areas are exact multiples of pi,
typed with a `π` key and offered as options that include the answer the
naive method gives.
