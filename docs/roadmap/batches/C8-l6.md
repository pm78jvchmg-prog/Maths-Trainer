# C8-l6: Inequalities & the Modulus Function: Piecewise Functions

Status: done
Branch: `claude/roadmap-c8-inequalities-l6`

Level `im-l6`, 5 lessons (Defining and Evaluating, Writing a Modulus in
Pieces, Sketching in Pieces, Continuity at the Joins, Solving with Pieces), a
15-question level check, 21 new generators (`mod-piece-*`, `mod-split-*`,
`mod-abs-cases-choice`, `mod-two-abs-tiles`, `mod-join-*`, `mod-jump`,
`mod-continuous-k`, `mod-k-solve-steps`), plus the derived
`mod-piece-value+choice`, `mod-jump+choice`, `mod-continuous-k+choice` and
`mod-piece-root+choice`. Every piece is a straight line on a whole-numbered
stretch; `piecewiseSvg` draws a jump with the owner's end filled and the other
hollow. The course test reads each rule off its `cases` block and each join's
dots off the picture, and works every value, jump, k and root out again.
