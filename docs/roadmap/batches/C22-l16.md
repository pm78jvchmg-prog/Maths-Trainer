# C22-l16: Contest Math: Finding and Counting Factors

Status: done
Branch: `cm-l16-work`

Level `cm-l16`: 3 lessons, a level check of 12 questions, 15 new generators in
`generators/contestFactors.ts`. A step on from Level 10's divisor counts and
HCF/LCM: the largest k with N a perfect k-th power is the HCF of its powers,
the smallest number with a digit product packs the primes into the largest
digits, and an equation of prime powers is two equations in the exponents.
A common divisor divides the difference (and 2^m − 1 runs Euclid on its
exponents), the pairs with a given LCM count 2e + 1 a prime, a gcd and a sum
leave a coprime split, and equal remainders shift a multiple of the LCM.
Divisors pair d with N/d, so only squares have an odd count, the product of
the divisors is N to half the count, and the divisors of N² pair around N.
Every answer is checked by brute force in a throwaway test, and every choice
form offers the trap: the smallest power, the primes as digits, the
difference itself, the divisor count for the LCM pairs, every split of the sum.
